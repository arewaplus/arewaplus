01 — INTRODUCTION

Welcome to ArewaPlus

ArewaPlus ("we", "our", "us") operates the ArewaPlus mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our App.

By using ArewaPlus, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

We do not sell your personal information. We never have, and we never will.

02 — DATA

Information We Collect

Information from Google Sign-In

When you sign in with Google, we receive and store:

  • Your name (display name)
  • Your email address
  • Your Google profile photo URL
  • A unique user identifier (Firebase Authentication UID)

You can use ArewaPlus as a guest without signing in, but some features (watchlist sync, cloud preferences) require sign-in.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Watch history — titles you started watching and your progress
  • Watchlist — titles you saved to "My List"
  • In-app search queries
  • Download history — metadata about titles you downloaded
  • App preferences — theme, streaming quality, autoplay settings
  • Basic app interaction data — screens visited, features used

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Your contacts, calendar, or SMS
  • Your photos or videos (other than the Google photo URL)
  • Your microphone or camera data
  • Your financial or payment information
  • Your health, fitness, or biometric data
03 — USAGE

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's core features
  • Personalise your experience — recommendations, continue watching
  • Sync your watchlist, watch history, and preferences across devices
  • Enable offline viewing — downloads stored on your device
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve app performance
  • Communicate important updates about the App
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your data, run ads against it, share it with data brokers, or track you across other apps.

05 — SECURITY

Storage & Security

Where Your Data Is Stored

  • Account information and preferences — stored in Firebase (Google Cloud)
  • Watch history, watchlist — stored locally AND synced to Firebase when signed in
  • Downloaded videos — stored ONLY on your device, in a private folder inaccessible to other apps

How We Protect Your Data

  • All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Firebase services use industry-standard encryption at rest
  • We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties
  • Access to user data is restricted to essential functions only

Data On Your Device

Content you download for offline viewing is stored in a private folder within the App's data directory. Uninstalling the App permanently removes all downloaded content and locally stored data.

Guest data stays on the device. Signing out switches you back to guest mode with separate data. Signing back in with the same Google account restores your synced data.

Children's Privacy

ArewaPlus is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

06 — RETENTION

Data Retention

How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Account information — retained while your account is active
  • Watch history, watchlist, preferences — retained while your account is active
  • Upon account deletion — all personal data removed within 30 days
  • Anonymous analytics — retained for up to 90 days
  • Server security logs — retained for up to 30 days

Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will process your request within 30 days.

07 — RIGHTS

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information in your profile
  • Delete specific data without deleting your account
  • Delete your entire account and all associated data
  • Request a copy of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

How to Exercise These Rights

You can manage your data directly in the App:

  • Profile → Clear watch history — remove all Continue Watching entries
  • My List → remove items — delete individual titles
  • Downloads → delete — remove offline files
  • Profile → Sign Out — switch to guest mode

For full account deletion or data export, email us at arewaplus001@gmail.com.

08 — OFFLINE

Downloads & Offline Content

When you download a movie or episode:

  • The video file is saved to your device's private app storage
  • No one else can access these files
  • The files are automatically removed if you uninstall the App
  • Download metadata is stored locally and synced to your account if signed in
  • Deleting the download in-app removes both the file and its metadata

We recommend downloading only over Wi-Fi to avoid data charges from your mobile carrier.

09 — GLOBAL
