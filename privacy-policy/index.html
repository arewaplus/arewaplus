Privacy Policy for ArewaPlus

Effective Date: September 21, 2026

ArewaPlus ("we", "our", or "us") operates the ArewaPlus mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

a. Personal Information

  • User ID (generated via Firebase Authentication)
  • Name and email address (if you choose to sign in with Google)
  • Profile photo URL (from your Google account)

b. Usage Data

  • App activity (movies watched, search history, and preferences)
  • Watch history and continue watching progress
  • Saved titles in My List
  • Download history (metadata only — video files stay on your device)
  • Device information (device type, operating system, app version)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Stream movies and series and personalize content
  • Save your watchlist, watch history, and preferences
  • Enable offline downloads
  • Analyze app performance and user behavior
  • Prevent fraud and abuse

3. Third-Party Services

ArewaPlus uses trusted third-party services, including:

  • Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage) — for accounts and data storage
  • Bunny Stream — for video streaming and downloads
  • Google Sign-In — for account creation and login

These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With service providers (Firebase, Bunny Stream, Google)
  • If required by law or legal processes
  • To protect our rights and users

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. All data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Downloads & Offline Content

Videos you download for offline viewing are stored only on your device, in a private folder that other apps cannot access. Uninstalling the app will permanently remove all downloaded content.

7. Children's Privacy

ArewaPlus is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access or delete your data
  • Clear your watch history or saved items at any time
  • Sign out and use the app as a guest
  • Stop using the app at any time

For any requests, contact us using the information below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app or on this page.
