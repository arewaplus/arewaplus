Delete Your ArewaPlus Account

Last updated: September 21, 2026

You can request deletion of your ArewaPlus account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email from the Google account you used to sign in to ArewaPlus:

  1. Email us at arewaplus001@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Delete My Account
  3. Include your Google email address in the message
Email
arewaplus001@gmail.com

Delete Data Without Deleting Your Account

You can delete some of your data without deleting your entire account using the options below:

  • Watch history — go to Profile → Clear watch history
  • Saved titles (My List) — tap ✕ on any item to remove it
  • Downloads — tap delete on any download, or use "Delete all"

To delete all data associated with your account, email us at arewaplus001@gmail.com.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, profile photo URL)
  • Your watch history and continue watching progress
  • Your saved titles (My List)
  • Your download metadata
  • Your app preferences and settings

What Data Is Kept

  • Anonymous analytics — up to 90 days
  • Server security logs — up to 30 days

Processing Time

We will process your request and permanently delete your account and data within 30 days.

Data on Your Device

Videos you downloaded for offline viewing are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the ArewaPlus app will remove all downloaded content automatically.
